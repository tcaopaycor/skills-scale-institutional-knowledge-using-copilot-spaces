# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

This README provides an overview and links to all project management process documentation for OctoAcme. These docs define the end-to-end lifecycle of project delivery, from initiation through retrospective, along with the roles and personas involved.

---

## Summary of OctoAcme Project Management Processes

OctoAcme follows a structured, lifecycle-based approach to project management that emphasizes clarity, collaboration, and continuous improvement. Projects begin with a formal **initiation** phase where goals, stakeholders, and success criteria are defined, followed by **planning** where work is decomposed into milestones and timelines established. During **execution and tracking**, progress is monitored through standups, sprint reviews, and project boards to ensure delivery stays on schedule and within scope.

**Risk management and communication** are treated as ongoing responsibilities throughout the project, with proactive identification of blockers and stakeholder updates delivered on a regular cadence. When work is ready for customers, the **release and deployment** process governs how changes are staged, validated, and shipped. After each major delivery, the team conducts **retrospectives** to capture lessons learned and feed improvements back into future iterations.

Across all phases, well-defined **roles and personas** — including Developers, Product Managers, and Project Managers — ensure accountability and clear ownership. These processes are designed to reduce single-person dependency risk, accelerate onboarding, and enable consistent, repeatable project execution across the organization.
